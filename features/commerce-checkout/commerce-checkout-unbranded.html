Case Overview

B2B SaaS commerce-first ordering flow

This portfolio case shows how a familiar commerce checkout pattern can be translated into a B2B SaaS ordering workflow with account setup, company validation, email verification, contract acceptance, payment authentication and order status transparency.

Design Focus
Guest modePublic discovery
B2B checkoutAccount & company first
Account activationPost-checkout
Status transparencyAfter payment
Portfolio framing: This unbranded mock-up focuses on interaction logic, validation states and customer journey structure. Detailed case study notes can live outside the prototype.

Public customer journey

1. Discover

Public product cards and FAQ explain what can be ordered before login.

2. Configure

The technical wizard collects product options before account setup.

3. Complete checkout

Account, company, billing and payment details are captured in one checkout; email verification follows after successful order submission.

4. Checkout & track

Contract acceptance, payment authentication and order status form one coherent completion flow.

Ordering & Account Activation

Do I need an account before ordering?
No. You can start as a guest. Account, company, billing and payment details are collected in checkout; email verification follows after successful order submission.
When does payment start?
Only after the company and billing details have passed validation, including sanctions screening.
Where can I see the provisioning status?
After successful checkout, the order overview shows the current order and activation status.

System View

Dev view: a sanctions hit during final checkout submit is presented to the customer as a neutral technical error. No payment or order object is created in the backend.

✓
Checkout submitted
The customer has submitted contact, account, company, billing and payment details. The sanctions check runs during final checkout validation before payment and final order creation continue.
!
Sanctions hit
The hit is detected during final checkout validation and blocks payment and final order creation.
◌
Do not create order or payment
Final order creation remains blocked and no final order is created.
◌
Do not forward payment data
No payment authentication is started and no payment data is sent to the payment provider.
◌
Do not send email
Neither the verification email, the welcome email, nor any order emails may be triggered.
Dev Note – Sanctions hit:
The hit must not be visible to the customer. The customer sees a neutral technical error and cannot continue to Checkout. Do not use customer-facing terms such as sanctions list, compliance, fraud, or review. Important: in this case, no outgoing email may be sent at all – neither verification email, welcome email, nor order emails.
