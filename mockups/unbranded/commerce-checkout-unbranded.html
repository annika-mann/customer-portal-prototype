Case Overview

B2B SaaS commerce-first ordering flow

This portfolio case shows how a familiar commerce checkout pattern can be translated into a B2B SaaS ordering workflow with company validation, contract acceptance, payment method verification and post-purchase account activation.

Portfolio framing: This mock-up translates a familiar B2C checkout pattern into a B2B SaaS workflow with validation, contract acceptance, payment method verification and provisioning transparency.

FAQ

Public help for guest customers before login and account activation.

Common questions

Do I need an account before ordering?
No. The order can start in guest mode. Account activation happens after order approval.
When is payment started?
Payment starts only after company and compliance validation have passed.
Why is there an order review step?
It separates technical configuration from checkout data and keeps the checkout shorter.
Where do I track provisioning?
After account activation, the order status page shows provisioning progress.
