Product framing

Goal
Translate a familiar commerce checkout pattern into a B2B SaaS ordering workflow.
Signals
New customers can configure a product, complete account setup, verify email, accept contracts, authenticate payment and understand order status without manual handover.
Potential metrics
Checkout completion rate, validation error rate, duplicate-handling rate, payment authentication success, time to order status visibility.

Dev view: a sanctions hit during the company data pre-check is presented to the customer as a neutral technical error.

✓
Account & company details submitted
The customer has submitted contact, account, company, and billing details. The sanctions pre-check runs before Checkout & Payment is enabled.
!
Sanctions hit
The hit is detected during the company data pre-check and blocks the transition to Checkout & Payment.
◌
Do not enable checkout
Checkout & Payment remains blocked and no final order is created.
◌
Do not start payment
No payment authentication is started and no payment data is sent to the payment provider.
◌
Do not send email
Neither the verification email, the welcome email, nor any order emails may be triggered.
Dev Note – Sanctions hit:
The hit must not be visible to the customer. The customer sees a neutral technical error and cannot continue to Checkout & Payment. Do not use customer-facing terms such as sanctions list, compliance, fraud, or review. Important: in this case, no outgoing email may be sent at all – neither verification email, welcome email, nor order emails.
